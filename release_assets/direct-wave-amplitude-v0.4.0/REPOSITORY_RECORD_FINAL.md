## Observable and normalization

The analyzed waveform quantity is the SXS ExtCCE Bondi-news
`(ell,m)=(2,2)` harmonic. The manuscript denotes by `M` the SXS ExtCCE
system-mass normalization: the combined Christodoulou mass of the two black
holes measured at the SXS reference time. No additional amplitude
renormalization is applied.
