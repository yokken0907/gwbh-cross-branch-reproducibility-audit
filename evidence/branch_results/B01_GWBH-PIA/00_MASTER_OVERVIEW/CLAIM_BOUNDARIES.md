# Claim Boundaries

## Supported
- Frozen release-drift ranks tracked posterior release-drift ranks.
- Frozen fixed-release summary model-spread ranks tracked posterior public-analysis-chain dependence ranks.
- The summary metric functioned as an ordinal audit-prioritization tool within the eligible 31-event frame.
- Ranks 1–7 all showed broad direct posterior dependence.
- Tier B boundary cases attenuated from rank 8 to rank 13.

## Not Supported or Not Attempted
- Pure waveform-model error measurement.
- Ranking the physically correct model.
- Black-hole new physics or observational anomaly.
- Causal attribution fractions to priors, approximants, remnant mappings, samplers, or metadata.
- Unconditional generalization beyond the audited frame.
- Tier C direct audit.
- Population, ringdown, GR-test, or standard-siren propagation.
