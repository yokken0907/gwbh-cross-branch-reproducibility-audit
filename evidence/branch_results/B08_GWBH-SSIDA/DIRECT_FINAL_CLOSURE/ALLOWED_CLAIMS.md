# Allowed claims

- **A01** — The frozen GWTC-5 public H0 outputs exhibit measurable posterior differences across several published analysis choices, including pipeline, galaxy catalog, weighting, population model and event-selection/IFAR variants.
- **A02** — Within the audited FullPop validation comparisons, the IFAR variants produced larger descriptive posterior shifts/distances than the other frozen primary/robustness comparisons highlighted here.
- **A03** — Catalog, pipeline, weighting and population-model choices also produce non-zero public-output dependence, with magnitude varying by model/route.
- **A04** — The final spectral+GW170817 and dark+GW170817 public posterior samples are not identical; the frozen C10 comparison is a valid descriptive output comparison.
- **A05** — The preregistered Phase4 primary result is partial: 10 of 12 comparisons are valid and C11/C12 are public-output-limited because the bright-only artifact is grid+density rather than direct samples.
- **A06** — The release-documented GW170817 grid-density conversion can be reproduced as a deterministic post-hoc diagnostic, but it does not restore C11/C12 as preregistered primary results.
- **A07** — Population modeling is a direct methodological dependency of the published standard-siren H0 routes; Branch01/05/07 connect only partially or structurally under the frozen cross-branch map.
- **A08** — The project can identify public-output dependence and provenance boundaries without determining which H0 value is physically correct.
