# CLAIM BOUNDARY

## Supported

This master supports only the audit conclusions that: (1) the Foundation public assets and schemas were audited under their recorded contracts; (2) production lineage requirements L01–L10 remained unresolved in the final frozen snapshot; (3) the two L07 review candidates were adjudicated non-resolving rather than promoted to production-exact evidence; and (4) the branch therefore stopped under `NO_GO_PUBLIC_ASSETS_INSUFFICIENT` without numerical Phase 4 work.

## Not supported

This package does **not** support a claim about the true numerical selection efficiency, found mask, pdet, VT, ESS, the astrophysical population, waveform-model superiority, anomalous black-hole physics, new physics, or the absolute nonexistence of unpublished / private / future production provenance.

GWTC-5 and A10 are outside this branch closure and were not used to create a numerical result.
