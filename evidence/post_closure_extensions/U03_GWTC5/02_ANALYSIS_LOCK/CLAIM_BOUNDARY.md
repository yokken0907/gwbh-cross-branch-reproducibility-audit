# Claim boundary

This artifact is an independent post-closure, post-summary-unblinding, candidate-posterior-blind retrospective calibration. It is not a repair or continuation of historical RUN01 and it does not alter the project Master, B07, B04, or any historical preregistration or closure.

The maximum allowed scientific statement is whether the semantically corrected public-summary score does or does not positively rank the frozen full-posterior model-distance response across all 22 events in the exact GWTC-5.0 release, exact model triad, and exact common-5 parameter set.

The following claims are forbidden regardless of the numerical result:

- strict or independent replication/non-replication of B01 or B07;
- correctness, truth, reliability, or superiority of any waveform model;
- attribution of a difference purely to waveform systematics;
- population, selection-function, remnant/ringdown, or cosmological propagation;
- tests or violations of GR, Kerr, no-hair, anomalies, or new physics;
- generalization beyond the exact release, 22 events, three models, five parameters, metric, and frozen test;
- treating the public notebook/data mismatch as an official LVK erratum.

The predictor and response share the same released posterior products. A supported result would therefore establish only release-specific screening calibration, not external physical validation.
