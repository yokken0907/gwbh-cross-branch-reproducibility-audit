# Reproducibility scope

## Included

- deterministic verification of final branch closures and cross-branch classifications;
- exact B01 RUN13/RUN21 confirmatory packages;
- exact integrated Project Master with all eight canonical branch Masters;
- all 21 B08 large posterior payloads required by the final primary/robustness calculations;
- executable B08 recomputation for C01–C10, R01–R17, and D01–D02;
- preservation of the C11/C12 primary public-output limitation;
- exact reconstruction metadata for the two original B08 evidence-companion ZIPs;
- frozen source/provenance records for upstream public assets.
- deterministic verification of the B04/U02 post-closure gate decision;
- derived outputs, frozen contracts, source, tests, and independent replay records for U03 and XR01;
- separately self-verifying A03/A04 review archives and exact event-level source values for the publication figure.

## Not duplicated

Superseded exploratory RUN archives and repeated starter/result copies are not duplicated merely to preserve chronology. They are not required to regenerate the final reported audit state.

Upstream third-party public datasets are generally referenced by frozen release identity, DOI/URL, and branch provenance rather than copied wholesale. The repository extracts omit the upstream GWTC-5.0 and GWTC-4.1 notebook/HDF5 files; source-level U03/XR01 reruns therefore reacquire official public files using frozen identities and delete raw event HDFs after derived-output persistence.

## Distinction

This repository targets scientific-result reproduction and auditability. Integrity checks, byte-for-byte reconstruction, and numerical agreement do not by themselves establish physical truth or causal attribution.

The post-closure calibration and transport are within-product association tests. Their support does not promote any historical cross-branch edge to `DIRECT_NUMERIC` and does not authorize same-data rescue or exploratory variant searches.
